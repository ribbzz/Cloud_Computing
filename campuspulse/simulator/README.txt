Run from your computer with Python 3.12 or newer; no third-party packages needed.

python3 simulate.py --api https://cloud.learningtheory.xyz/api --user sensor.fleet --rate 1 --duration 60 --anomaly 0.3

Enter the device password at the hidden prompt. No default passwords are provided.
The admin role can also ingest; the October 8 demonstration used ops.admin.
Keep passwords out of command-line arguments and screenshots.
